About Us

Your Supply Chain. Our Commitment.

At Unlimited Carrier, our story began in 1999 with just three drivers, three trucks, and one clear mission: to deliver freight with purpose, precision, and pride.

From Three Trucks to a Nationwide Fleet

What started as a small team has grown into a nationwide fleet serving major brands and retailers across the United States. As part of the UC Group portfolio, Unlimited Carrier brings dependable capacity, experienced drivers, and quality equipment to every shipment.

We specialize in full truckload and time-sensitive shipments, combining advanced technology with personalized service to keep your supply chain running smoothly.
